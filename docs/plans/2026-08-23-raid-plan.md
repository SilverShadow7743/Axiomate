## Details most likely to be got wrong

- **`raidProblem` runs on the PATCH, before the spread** — validating the merged record
  would refuse an unrelated edit to a record whose stored values were fine.
- **Exposure is never stored** — the proof asserts the columns hold likelihood and impact
  only.
- **Recognition by stable id through the live registry** — `workTypeId(label)` on the
  ISSUE's label would break the moment the label is renamed; resolve label → registry
  entry → id.
- **The seed skips an already-discovered id** — a source log containing "Risk" must not
  produce two WT_RISK entries.
- **The wire widens with the reducer** — third outing of the 828f975 lesson.
- **FAIL gates parse JSON** — python, utf-8 stdout.
